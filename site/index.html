Data: CC BY 4.0. Code: MIT. Cite as Percy, J. Freeze Ledger (dated release).

Attributions are the author's assessment of public records (OFAC SDN entries, Federal Register notices, DOJ filings, issuer statements), not the issuer's stated reason for a freeze. "No public record found" means exactly that, not that none exists. Addresses the issuers blacklist for internal reasons (their own contracts) are kept in the data, flagged, and excluded from the counts above.
